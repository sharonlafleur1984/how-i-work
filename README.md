# How I work

**Last updated:** October 6, 2026

I'm Sharon LaFleur, a product designer with about 8 years of experience. This is how I design and build products with AI

**My design philosophy:**
- Keep it as simple as possible.
- Use progressive disclosure (show what matters most and tuck away the rest until it's needed).
- Sprinkle in a little delight, with annimation, motion, or copy. Used sparingly, with taste, and only when it adds to the usability.
